⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. One spot is at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, and the guides flag it as possibly only available during Chapter 1. Meditate there the first chance you get, before you finish Chapter 1. Everything else on the list can be earned after the story, in free roam. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — and never use a cheat on your platinum save: cheats disable trophies and saving.

Sources & honesty notes

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the ones shown on the console trophy list (PS5 / Xbox), as compiled from the independent guides below. No official Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft page publishes the list as a web page, and the guides cross-check each other — they agree on all 55 names and grades. Each trophy card names the guides its own steps rest on.

    What is verified, what is not. 47 trophies are confirmed: the guides agree on how to earn them. 6 are reported: the guides agree on a likely method drawn from the trophy description, but not every source has confirmed it. 2 — Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There — are unconfirmed: no published guide states a method as of 21 November 2026, and this guide says so instead of inventing one. One disagreement: Push Square’s page labels Maximum Withdrawal Bronze, while GTA Vibes, GTA Scoop, KeenGamer and the kit data list it Silver; this guide lists it Silver.

    Guide text is written for this page in our own words; it draws on the guides above and cites them, and never copies their text. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used. This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
